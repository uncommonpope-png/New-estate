SEVEN CALCULATIONS
The Brief

The industry profits from the gap between what you know and what is true about your own business.

NUMBER 01
Your Actual Net Per Transaction
The gross commission is the number the industry shows you. The net per transaction is the number your business actually runs on.
The $9,200 commission and the $3,800 you actually keep are not the same career.
NUMBER 02
The Referral Rate Your Practice Is Capable Of
The documented referral rate of systematically maintained client relationships is 65 percent. The average agent generates 18 to 22 percent.
The referrals that don't come are invisible. The cost of their absence is not.
NUMBER 03
What Your Brokerage Actually Makes From You
The fully-loaded brokerage cost for most agents is 38 to 45 percent of gross commission, not the 25 to 30 percent the split implies.
The split they quote and the amount you keep are two different numbers.
NUMBER 04
The Tax You Are Not Required To Pay
Four decisions save $25,000 to $40,000 annually. Every year. Without earning one additional dollar.
You have already earned this money. The question is whether you keep it.
NUMBER 05
The Full Cost of Your Worst Client This Year
The transaction that produced $9,200 in gross commission frequently produced a net loss of $3,000 to $8,000 in True Value.
The decline is the most expensive free thing in the business.
NUMBER 06
What Your Database Is Actually Worth
The three-year value difference between maintained and unmaintained database: $121,600.
Your database is an asset. The question is whether you are managing it like one.
NUMBER 07
Your Runway - The Number That Determines Everything
The agent with two months of runway and the agent with eight months of runway are not having the same conversations.
The client reads the runway in the first ninety seconds.
